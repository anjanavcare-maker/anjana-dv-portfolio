FAQ

The questions worth asking.

How is this different from the website package?

The website package is a one-time build that ends at launch. The Growth Partnership keeps going: search visibility, content, Google Business Profile, campaigns and reporting are worked on every month and reviewed with you.

What does it cost?

₹20,000 per month, with the website design and build included. Advertising spend is separate and paid directly to Google or Meta.

Is advertising spend included?

No. The fee covers strategy, management and production. Ad spend is paid by you directly to the platform, so you keep full control and can see exactly where the money goes.

How long before I see results?

Paid campaigns can produce enquiries within days. SEO is slower — usually three to six months before rankings and organic enquiries move meaningfully. Anyone promising faster organic results is guessing.

Can you guarantee first-page Google rankings?

No, and no honest provider can. Google decides rankings. What can be controlled is the work that improves the odds: technical health, useful content, a complete Google Business Profile, reviews and quality links.

Do I need to be on every channel?

No. Channels are chosen around where your customers actually look and compare. A study abroad consultancy and a Dubai contractor need very different mixes.

I already have a website. Can you still help?

Yes. Existing sites are audited first. If the foundation is sound, the work goes into search, content and conversion. If it is holding results back, the rebuild is folded into the plan.

Is there a minimum term?

Three months, because search and content work needs time to compound. After that it continues month to month with no lock-in.
