FAQ

Questions clients ask before we start.

How long does a website take?

Once you provide all your content and information, delivery happens in minimum time — most projects go live within days, not weeks.

What do you need from me to start?

Text, images, your logo, and a clear idea of the pages you want. The more complete the information, the faster the build — use the planner above to map it out in five taps.

Do I get a domain and hosting?

Yes — I set up your domain and hosting as part of the project, and hand everything over so you fully own your website.

Can I edit the site myself afterwards?

Every site is built on WordPress, so yes. You can update text, images and pages without touching code — and I'm always here for support.

Can you integrate payments on my store?

Yes. For e-commerce I set up WooCommerce with UPI, card and Razorpay payment integration, plus order and inventory management.

What support do I get after launch?

Launch support, handover training, and ongoing care plans to keep the site fast, secure and current as your business grows.
